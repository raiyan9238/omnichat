## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Core Philosophy
The design system powers an enterprise-grade, omni-channel conversational AI appointment orchestrator designed for clinics, medical practices, high-end spas, and wellness institutions. The core personality is anchored in **clinical precision, executive authority, absolute transparency, and effortless immediacy**. It bridges the divide between algorithmic speed and the reassuring empathy expected of top-tier medical and wellness concierge desks.

Users—ranging from busy clinic receptionists and practice administrators to inbound patients and high-value clients—must experience an environment where zero ambiguity exists. The UI avoids the gimmicky tropes of experimental AI bots; instead, it adopts an aesthetic of **systemic certainty**, where live calendar states, latency indicators, buffer validations, and human handoffs operate with surgical clarity.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Direction
The visual architecture blends **Modern Corporate SaaS** with **Precision Clinical Minimalism**:
- **Structured Precision**: Hairline surface borders, rigorous grid alignment, and high tabular data readability ensure operational density without visual clutter.
- **Ambient Focus & Pulse**: Crisp, light slate surfaces form the foundational canvas, while deliberate accents—such as Royal Blue for primary intent and AI Teal for live voice streaming/telephony pulses—guide attention to active state changes.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Transparency**: Handoff cards, confidence meters, and pre-booking summaries feature explicit visual boundaries and clear categorical color coding (Confirmed Green, Warning Amber, Escalation Crimson).

## Colors

### Hierarchy & Role Distribution
The palette creates a medical-grade visual environment that satisfies WCAG AAA contrast for body typography and high-density tabular data:

- **Primary (`#0F172A` - Deep Slate)**: Anchors global system structures, dark sidebar contexts, persistent navigation, primary headlines, and authoritative modal headers.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB` - Royal Blue)**: Drives operational actions, active tabs, primary commit buttons, keyboard focus rings, and high-confidence slot availability selections.
- **Tertiary (`#0D9488` - AI Teal)**: Serves as the AI intelligence pulse. Used for live transcription streaming indicators, voice telephony wave status, web widget presence, and verified synchronization states.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` - Slate Neutral)**: Manages secondary text, timestamps, system metadata, inactive tab indicators, and empty state iconography.

### Specialized Omni-Channel & Status Mapping
To deliver instant recognition in the live omni-channel activity stream and session desks, distinct channel tokens are codified:
- **Voice Telephony**: Badged with `badge-voice-bg` (`#F5F3FF`) and text/border `channel-voice` (`#7C3AED`) to highlight real-time audio streams.
- **Meta Messenger**: Badged with `badge-messenger-bg` (`#EFF6FF`) and `channel-messenger` (`#0084FF`).
- **Instagram Direct**: Badged with `badge-instagram-bg` (`#FDF2F8`) and `channel-instagram` (`#E1306C`).
- **Webchat Engine**: Badged with `badge-web-bg` (`#F0FDFA`) and `channel-web` (`#0D9488`).

Operational states use standard semantic signals:
- **Confirmed / Booked**: `#16A34A`
- **Slot Conflict / Buffer Warning**: `#F59E0B`
- **Escalated Handoff / API Offline**: `#DC2626`

## Typography

### Structural Pairings & Rationale
Typography is structured around a dual-engine model:
1. **Plus Jakarta Sans (Display & Headlines)**: Brings modern executive clarity and contemporary warmth to high-level headers, card titles, and conversion sections.
2. **Inter (Interface & Body)**: Selected for its neutral legibility, tall x-height, and robust micro-rendering characteristics in dense scheduling matrices, split-pane conversation transcripts, and form control groups.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Technical Badges & Metrics)**: Reserved for API latency metrics (`P95 1.1s`), timestamps, booking reference hashes, entity extraction tokens (`[Slot: Nov 28, 2:30 PM]`), and calendar synchronization logs.

### Application Guidelines
- **Form Controls & Modals**: Headers in modals use `headline-sm` with high tracking control. Field labels rely on `label-md` uppercase styling with subtle tracking (`0.02em`) to maintain clinical precision.
- **Real-Time Transcripts**: Conversation bubbles use `body-md` for patient/caller dialogue, interspersed with `label-mono` inline pills for AI entity captures.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Architecture & Grid Behavior
The system relies on an **8px strictly incremental rhythm**, mapping seamlessly to both marketing pages and multi-panel operational consoles.

1. **Business Admin & Live Desk**:
   - Fixed-to-collapsible sidebar navigation (64px icon-only rail expanding to 240px contextual menu).
   - Fixed 56px sticky top bar hosting multi-location selectors, global Command-K search, and real-time scheduler health indicators.
   - Fluid 12-column operational workspace utilizing `gutter` (1.5rem) and standard container margins (2rem). In the Live Desk view, this transforms into a 3-column split-pane:
     - Left: Session Queue (300px fixed width).
     - Center: Live Streaming Transcript & Handoff View (flexible min 520px).
     - Right: Client Context & Calendar Commit Drawer (360px fixed width).
2. **Public Marketing Experience**:
   - 1200px max-width centered container with fluid edge gutters down to 16px on mobile viewports.
   - Dual-viewport hero container featuring a 50/50 desktop split (Value Proposition left / Live Simulator Engine right), reflowing to a vertical single-column stack below 1024px.

### Breakpoints & Reflow Directives
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: Sidebar shifts to an off-canvas drawer. Split-pane operational views collapse into single active-tab panels with bottom-sheet modals for scheduling actions. Primary touch targets maintain a minimum 44px hit-box.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: Left navigation defaults to compact 64px rail mode. 4-column metric grids reflow to 2x2 matrices.
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: Full multi-column dashboard orchestration with simultaneous visibility of streaming logs and calendar commit verification cards.

## Elevation & Depth

### Surface Layering Philosophy
Visual hierarchy is defined through **Tonal Layering** accompanied by **Subtle Hairline Borders (`#E2E8F0`)** rather than aggressive dropshadows. This conveys a clinical, high-integrity dashboard feel.

1. **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#F8FAFC`. The foundational backdrop for the entire dashboard viewport and landing page canvas.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Container Tier)**: `#FFFFFF` bounded by a 1px solid border (`#E2E8F0`). Used for operational panels, metrics cards, table rows, and conversation streams.
3. **Level 2 (Floating & Interactive Surfaces)**: `#FFFFFF` with a crisp ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Applied to dropdown panels, date-picker flyouts, and pre-booking summary cards.
4. **Level 3 (Modals & Escalation Overlays)**: `#FFFFFF` with high-diffusion depth: `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`, complemented by a 40% opacity slate backdrop overlay (`#0F172A`).

### Active AI Pulses
Interactive AI states avoid heavy skeuomorphic glows. Instead, a dual-ring ambient teal micro-pulse is applied:
- `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.15)` on live microphone waveforms and active text ingestion badges.

## Shapes

### Corner Curvature Rules
The design system employs **Roundedness Level 2 (0.5rem / 8px baseline)**, striking an intentional balance between enterprise software durability and modern digital friendliness:

- **Base Elements (0.5rem / 8px)**: Standard buttons, text input fields, table filter selects, KPI metric cards, and calendar timeline slots.
- **Containers & Modals (1rem / 16px - `rounded-lg`)**: Live Desk split panels, main dashboard overview containers, marketing feature cards, and pre-booking review dialogs.
- **Pills & Status Indicators (Full Radius / 9999px)**: Omnichannel source badges (Voice, Messenger, IG, Web), entity extraction tokens, confidence score pills, and AI pulse indicators.

## Components

### 1. Omni-Channel Badges & Channel Tags
- **Structure**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) flex badges containing an SVG channel icon and a JetBrains Mono text descriptor.
- **Styling**:
  - *Voice*: Background `#F5F3FF`, Text `#6D28D9`, Border `rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.2)`.
  - *Messenger*: Background `#EFF6FF`, Text `#1D4ED8`, Border `rgba(0, 132, 255, 0.2)`.
  - *Instagram*: Background `#FDF2F8`, Text `#BE185D`, Border `rgba(225, 48, 108, 0.2)`.
  - *Webchat*: Background `#F0FDFA`, Text `#0F766E`, Border `rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.2)`.
- **States**: When active/streaming, includes a 6px circular dot with a CSS pulse animation.

### 2. Action Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Background `#2563EB`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `Inter` 600 weight, 8px radius. Hover: `#1D4ED8`. Active: scale `0.98`. Focus ring: 3px `#2563EB` at 25% opacity.
- **Human Take-Over (Emergency Handoff)**: Background `#DC2626`, text `#FFFFFF`, icon: headset/hand icon. Hover: `#B91C1C`.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, text `#1E293B`. Hover: `#F8FAFC`, border `#CBD5E1`.

### 3. Pre-Booking Confirmation Card
- **Layout**: Self-contained card nested inside chat transcripts and modal windows.
- **Structure**: Header bar with Deep Slate text (`headline-sm`), two-column grid displaying Location, Service Name, Provider, Slot Time, and Price.
- **Notice Bar**: Light amber background (`#FFFBEB`) with 12px caption: "Cancellation Policy: Cancel up to 24 hours prior without fee."
- **Footer**: Dual action layout with "Modify Details" (Secondary) and "Confirm & Book Slot" (Primary Blue).

### 4. Interactive Live Calendar Timeline Slot
- **Visuals**: Distinct cell height (48px per hour increment).
- **Slot States**:
  - *AI Locked / Reserved*: `#F0FDFA` background with 2px dashed border `#0D9488`, showing active patient name and omni-channel badge.
  - *Confirmed Committed*: Solid `#FFFFFF`, left border 4px solid `#16A34A`, shadow level 1.
  - *Slot Conflict / Buffer Warning*: Background `#FEF3C7`, left border 4px solid `#F59E0B`.

### 5. Input Fields & Search (Cmd+K)
- **Styling**: Height 40px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, text `#1E293B`, placeholder `#94A3B8`, radius 8px.
- **Focus**: Border `#2563EB`, outline 3px solid `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.

### 6. KPI Metric Cards
- **Structure**: White card container, 16px padding.
- **Top Row**: Label (`label-md` muted) with an auxiliary channel or trend icon.
- **Middle Row**: High-contrast figure (`headline-lg` in `#0F172A`).
- **Bottom Row**: Micro-trend badge (`label-mono-sm`) with green/red pill for period-over-period percentage shifts.
